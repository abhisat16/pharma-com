How it works

Speak. Check. Say “haan”.

01

Speak

Name, age, medicine, quantity. Any order, any of five languages, mixed if you like.

“Pan-D 2 strip aur Cetzine 5 goli, Rahul 30 saal”
02

Check

The bill appears with stock, rack, batch and expiry. Out of stock or expired? It offers the same salt.

Crocin out of stock → Calpol 500 · same salt
03

Confirm

Say “haan”, “ho”, “haudu” or “avunu”. The bill prints and stock updates on its own.

Billed in 9 s · Dolo 650 now 38 strips
What the owner gets

Billing, stock and expiry in one voice.

Five Indian languages

Hindi, English, Marathi, Kannada, Telugu. Numbers spoken as words work too.

Live stock

“Dolo 650 stock mein hai?” answers with quantity and rack. “20 strip add karo” restocks.

Expiry first

Stock sorted by expiry. Filter 30 days to 1 year. Expired stock is blocked at billing.

Owner dashboard

Sales, top sellers and a “sell first” list of stock about to expire, with its value.

Safer dispensing

Rx (Schedule H) flags, same-salt substitutes, short-stock warnings before you confirm.

Proper invoices

GST-style bill with batch and expiry per line. Every bill and voice command is logged.
